### P2 — Shading math: shadow bias, contact darkening, AO, and sky occlusion

**Files owned (exclusive):** `src/render/Glsl.ts`, `src/render/MaterialPatch.ts`, `src/render/passes/GtaoPass.ts`
*P2 must not touch `World.ts` (P1 owns the ambient constant) or `Renderer.ts` (P3). Read trap 2 before starting — you own both halves of the "nothing meets the ground" contradiction.*

**Findings:**

> **`src/render/Glsl.ts:394-395` — blocker.** `float slope = clamp(tan(acos(clamp(NdotL,0.02,1.0))), 0.0, 6.0); float biasM = texelW * (0.85 + 1.25 * slope);` plus a normal offset `nOff = 1.1 + 2.2*(1-NdotL)` at **line 485**. Cascade texels read live at ultra/2048: 0.92 cm (≤5.06 m), 1.98 cm (≤11.24 m), 4.93 cm (≤27.86 m), 25.0 cm (≤140 m). On flat ground at 18:00 NdotL = 0.191 → slope 5.15 → `biasM = texelW × 7.29` = **6.7 / 14.4 / 35.9 cm / 1.82 m** per cascade, plus normal offsets of 2.6 / 5.7 / 14.2 / 72 cm. Past 11 m nothing under 36 cm casts; past 28 m nothing under 1.8 m casts. A 4× crop of a czech hedgehog at 32° sun with all casters enabled is a formless grey smear with no beam structure. *Fix:* `clamp(tan(acos(NdotL)), 0.0, 2.0)` **and** cap absolute bias, `biasM = min(texelW*(0.85+1.25*slope), 0.06)`; cap `nOff` at ~1.8.
